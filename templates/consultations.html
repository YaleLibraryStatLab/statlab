What to Expect

Our consultants are available to help with all aspects of quantitative research — from study design and power analysis to model selection, interpretation of results, and data visualization. Sessions are collaborative and tailored to your project.

You don't need to have a fully-formed question. Bring whatever you're working on — a dataset, an analysis script, a draft methods section — and we'll work through it together.

How to Book

  1. Browse consultants — visit the team page to see each consultant's expertise and the statistical methods they work with.
  2. Schedule a session — book directly below, or reach us at statlab@yale.edu with questions.
  3. Come prepared — bring a brief description of your project, any data or code you're working with, and your specific question if you have one.

Who Can Use StatLab?

Consultations are free and open to all current Yale students, faculty, postdocs, and staff. We support projects across all disciplines — from social sciences and public health to the biological and physical sciences, humanities, and professional schools.

Not sure where to start? Send us a note and we'll match you with the right consultant.
