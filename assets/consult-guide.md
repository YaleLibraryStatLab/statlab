# StatLab Guide to Consultation Success

Yale StatLab provides statistics and computational support to all Yale University affiliates. This guide is designed to help researchers prepare for their upcoming conultation, it contains notes about what your consultation will entail, how to prepare, what to expect, and how to ensure a productive meeting.

## What to Expect

The first question a consultant is likely to ask you is what your research question is. Our consultants are well trained methodologists, but they may not be domain experts in every applied subject that arrives at the door, regardless of whether or not they come from a similar disciplinary background. Honing in on the research question and a few tractable hypotheses allows us to distill the subject's questions into first-order statistics questions.
